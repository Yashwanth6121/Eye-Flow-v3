What is actually running?

MediaPipe detects face and iris landmarks from camera frames. EyeFlow selects between calibrated iris positions and eye-direction model signals to estimate up, center, or down—not a precise screen cursor.

Video is processed in your browser, not uploaded by this app. Model files load from Google and jsDelivr. Calibration remains in memory and is cleared when you stop the camera. No analytics are installed.

This controls only this page. It cannot control Instagram or other apps. Accuracy, battery use, and accessibility suitability are unvalidated. Keep your head still; recalibrate after moving the device.
