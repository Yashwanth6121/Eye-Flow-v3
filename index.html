01 — THE IDEA

Reading, with
room to breathe.

EyeFlow explores a simple interaction: moving through content without reaching for a mouse or touching the screen.

“A glance is a signal.
A sustained hold is intent.”

The challenge is deciding when a look becomes an action. A dwell timer makes that decision visible and deliberate.

02 — THE INTERACTION

Look. Hold.
Move forward.

In this demonstration, the bottom gaze zone starts a continuous 20-second timer. Leaving that zone clears the timer. Reaching the threshold scrolls to the next section.

One completed hold.
One scroll action.

After a scroll, the system waits for center input before another hold can begin.

04 — THE NEXT QUESTION

Measure the
tradeoffs.

A long dwell favors deliberate interaction but slows navigation. A short dwell is faster but may trigger unwanted scrolling.

Usability needs evidence.
Not a bigger claim.

Next validation targets: false triggers, successful actions, calibration repeatability, and comfort across different devices and lighting.
